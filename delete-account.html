How to permanently delete your SickleMe Care+ account and data

You have the right to delete your SickleMe Care+ account and all associated personal health data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Steps to Delete Your Account

1

Open the SickleMe Care+ app on your device.

2

Navigate to the Profile or Settings tab.

3

Tap "Delete Account" at the bottom of the page.

4

Confirm the deletion when prompted. Your account will be permanently deleted immediately.

What Gets Deleted

The following data is permanently and immediately deleted upon account removal:

  • Your account credentials (email address, name)
  • All pain tracking entries
  • All mood logs
  • All medication and treatment records
  • All hydration entries

Nothing is retained after deletion. There is no recovery period.
