SickleMe Care+

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026

SickleMe Care+ ("we", "our", or "us") is a health tracking app designed to help individuals managing sickle cell disease. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Data We Collect

  • Name and email address — used for account creation and authentication
  • Health information: pain levels, mood logs, hydration entries, medication records
  • Device identifiers — used by Firebase for app functionality

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide core app features (tracking, reminders, insights)
  • To authenticate and manage your account
  • Your data is never sold or shared with third parties for advertising

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore), encrypted in transit using TLS. Firebase's infrastructure complies with industry security standards. We do not store sensitive health data on your device beyond what is needed for offline functionality.

4. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data at any time within the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data — see our Delete Account page
  • Contact us with any data concerns or requests

5. Third-Party Services

SickleMe Care+ uses Google Firebase for authentication and data storage. Firebase acts as our infrastructure provider and processes data solely to deliver the service. We do not integrate any advertising SDKs or third-party analytics platforms.

6. Children's Privacy

SickleMe Care+ is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
